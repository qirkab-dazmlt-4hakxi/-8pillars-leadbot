# GOAT Enterprise Preconstruction Intelligence

This layer coordinates the bid package rather than analyzing drawings,
specifications, contract terms, schedule and procurement independently.

## Specification intelligence

GOAT identifies requirement-bearing clauses and preserves provenance.

Requirement categories include:

- submittals
- materials/products
- execution
- testing
- quality assurance
- warranties
- mockups
- closeout
- delivery
- coordination

Extracted numeric values remain evidence only.

GOAT must never interpret a numeric value as the governing requirement
without source/context validation.

## Contract risk

Division 00/01 and contractual text is screened for commercial and legal
review conditions including:

- liquidated damages
- retainage
- pay-if-paid / pay-when-paid concepts
- notice deadlines
- change-order authorization
- indemnity
- insurance
- warranties
- delay limitations
- termination
- dispute clauses
- flow-down provisions

These findings are risk-screening signals, not legal advice.

## CPM scheduling

The scheduling engine supports:

- activity durations
- finish-to-start
- start-to-start
- finish-to-finish
- start-to-finish
- lag
- cycle detection
- forward pass
- backward pass
- total float
- critical-path identification

Schedule intelligence may support bid planning and procurement timing.

It does not replace a project-specific superintendent, scheduler or
licensed professional where one is required.

## Procurement intelligence

Each package can model:

- submittal preparation
- design/review
- fabrication
- shipping
- field buffer
- required-on-site date

GOAT calculates:

- total lead duration
- latest responsible release date
- days remaining
- long-lead status
- critical late status

Supplier quotes and current vendor information should supersede generic
assumptions when available.

## Scope compliance matrix

Every major scope may require evidence from one or more sources:

- drawings
- specifications
- addenda
- RFIs
- contract
- estimate
- quote
- schedule
- submittal

GOAT classifies each scope as:

- satisfied
- review required
- unresolved
- conflicting

Conflicting fingerprints never silently overwrite one another.

## Automatic RFI intelligence

GOAT produces RFI candidates only when evidence demonstrates:

- missing governing information
- conflicting source information
- unresolved scope

The RFI engine asks for clarification.

It does not invent the missing engineering answer.

## Bid readiness

The executive readiness layer aggregates:

- unresolved scope
- blocking RFIs
- procurement timing
- contractual risk
- critical-path exposure

Submission readiness remains fail-closed when modeled blocking conditions
exist.
